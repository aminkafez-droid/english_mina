Reminder

Which form, and why?

Read the table before you start.

FormUse it forExample
will + verba decision made now; an offer; a promise; a predictionI’ll carry that bag for you. I think it will rain.
will be + verb-ingan action in progress at a moment in the futureAt nine tomorrow I’ll be sitting in a lesson.
will have + past participle (V3)an action finished before a moment in the futureBy nine I’ll have eaten breakfast.
time wordsat / this time tomorrow → will be -ing
by / by the time / before → will have V3		This time tomorrow I’ll be flying. By Friday I’ll have landed.
by / untilby = not later than; until = up to that timeSend it by Friday. I’ll be here until Friday.
