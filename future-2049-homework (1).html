Two ways to talk about the future

Look at K’s week. The arrow shows the moment we are talking about.

On Monday, K will be travelling to Las Vegas. (The journey is in progress.)

By Friday, K will have arrived in Las Vegas. (The journey is finished.)

Future continuous

will be + verb-ing

Tomorrow at 8, K will be driving.

He won’t be sleeping.

Will he be working?

An action in progress at a time in the future.

Future perfect

will have + past participle

By 8, K will have arrived.

He won’t have finished.

Will he have found her?

An action finished before a time in the future.
