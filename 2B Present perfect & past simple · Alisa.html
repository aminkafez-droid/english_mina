3. Stella's story

Before you read: Stella is a model. Where do you think she has worked? What has she never done?

From a small town to Paris

Stella was born in a small town in the south of Italy. When she was a toddler, she loved dressing up in her mum's clothes. At fourteen she signed up for a modelling course. Her parents weren't keen on the idea, but she didn't give up.

She left home when she was eighteen and moved to Milan. She has lived there for seven years. Since then she has done over 200 photo shoots. She has already worked in Paris and London, but she has never been to New York. She has just come back from a shoot in London.

Last year she ran out of energy and took a three-month break. "I've learned to put up with long days," she says. "And I've always looked up to models who stay kind."

a · Gist: choose the best summary

b · Finished or unfinished time?

Click a time phrase from the text, then click the column.

✔ finished time → past simple

⏳ unfinished time → present perfect

c · Learn this! Complete the rules

She has never been to New York. Has she visited New York? (No) Can she go in the future? (Yes) Do we know when? (No)
She left home when she was eighteen. Is it finished? (Yes) Do we know when? (Yes)
She has lived in Milan for seven years. Does she live in Milan now? (Yes)
She has just come back from London. A long time ago or very recently? (Very recently)

b · Two truths and a lie

Say 3 sentences about you. Two are true, one is a lie. Your teacher asks questions and guesses the lie.
1. I've (ever / never / already)…  ·  2. I've never…  ·  3. Last year / When I was… I…
Example: I've been to Paris. I've never broken a bone. When I was ten, I won a dance competition.
