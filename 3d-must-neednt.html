4must or have to / has to?

mustmy own opinion or feeling
I must call my mum. I miss her.
have to / has toa rule, a law, somebody else decides
I have to wear a uniform. It’s a rule.
he / she / it → has to (She has to work). But must never changes: She must work. Questions: Do you have to…? Does he have to…?

B. What is the difference? Choose a or b.

1 I must do my homework.
a) It’s my own decision.   b) Somebody told me to.
2 I have to do my homework.
a) It’s my own decision.   b) Somebody told me to.
3 You must try this café!
a) It’s my opinion – it’s great.   b) There’s a rule about it.
4 Sara has to wear a uniform.
a) She wants to.   b) It’s the school rule.
5 Does he have to wear a helmet?
a) Is it a rule?   b) Does he want to?
