Teacher notes (Shift+T)

Aim: Lena recognises and says 10 bike words in simple chunks: This is a… / Bikes have got… / Cyclists need… / Who’s got…? Words come in two groups of five (easier first: wheel, bell, light, basket, lock; then brakes, gears, pump, safety vest, cyclist) with a game after each. Pictures are my own drawings (one garage scene), not textbook photos; Anna, Max and Mia are drawn avatars. No audio: model pronunciation yourself. Earlier results are unknown, so add 1–2 min of retrieval if needed. The song (PB 3.3, p. 35) is not included: sing it from your own audio at the end if time allows; its chunks forgot to bring / check / take are for recognition only.

Keys. Guess: brakes, pump, gears, light, safety vest. Sort: gears, bell, wheels, basket → Bikes have got; lock, pump, safety vest → Cyclists need. Mistakes: “Bikes has got lights”, “Cyclists needs a lock”, “a gears”, “Who’s have”. Who’s got: Anna, Max, Mia, Max.

Likely errors: has/have; a gears; These is; mixing up light and bell. Recast, then repeat the whole sentence.
