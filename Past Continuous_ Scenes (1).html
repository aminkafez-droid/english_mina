      3Past continuousHow and why we use it

      GLook at the examples

      Formwas / were + verb-ing
      It was snowing. They were walking.
      Negative and questionwasn’t / weren’t + verb-ing
      It wasn’t raining. Was the dog sleeping?
      1 · Set the sceneWe use the past continuous to describe the background of a story.It was getting dark. The wind was blowing.
      2 · A moment in the pastWe use it to say what was in progress at a particular time.At three o’clock it was snowing.
      3 · Two actions togetherWith the same subject we don’t repeat was / were.She was sitting on a rock and drawing the mountains.

      G1Scene or event?

      Does the sentence describe the background (scene) or something that happened (event)? Look at the verb form.

      1. It was snowing and a cold wind was blowing.
      2. A bird landed on the tent.
      3. The sun was setting behind the hills.
      4. The hiker opened her umbrella.
      5. Two children were playing near the lake.
      6. The dog jumped into the water.

      G2Check the rule

      True or false?

      1. We use the past continuous to describe what was happening around us at a moment in the past.
      2. It is the usual tense for a list of finished events: first… then… after that…
      3. We form it with was / were + verb-ing.
      4. When the subject is the same we usually don’t repeat was / were: He was sitting and eating.
      5. For one quick action (The dog jumped into the water) we use the past continuous.

      4PracticeControlled practice

      P1Past continuous: positive

      Put the verb in brackets into the past continuous.

      1. At ten o’clock the sun (shine) brightly.
      2. Two ducks (swim) near the shore.
      3. The hiker (walk) along the path.
      4. Her dog (run) next to her.
      5. Big flakes of snow (fall) slowly.
      6. The fisherman (sit) in his boat.
      7. We (cook) dinner on the fire.
      8. I (take) photos of the sunset.

      P2Past continuous: negative

      Use the negative form: wasn’t / weren’t + verb-ing.

      1. At 3 pm it (not rain). It was snowing.
      2. The children (not swim) because the water was cold.
      3. The dog (not sleep). He was watching the fire.
      4. At 10 am we (not wear) coats. It was hot.
      5. I (not listen) to music. I was listening to the birds.
      6. The wind (not blow) at seven. The air was still.

      P3Was or were?

      Choose was or were.

      1. the birds singing?
      2. What the hiker doing?
      3. it raining at ten o’clock?
      4. Where the dog sleeping?
      5. What the children eating?
      6. Who you talking to?

      P4Don’t repeat was / were

      Write only the -ing form. We don’t repeat was / were after and.

      1. A woman was sitting on a rock and (draw) the mountains.
      2. We were sitting by the fire and (eat) sausages.
      3. The dogs were running and (bark).
      4. The tourists were laughing and (take) photos.
      5. He was waving goodbye and (walk) away.

      P5A morning at the lake

      Set the scene: put the verbs into the past continuous.

      Read the whole story first. Then fill in the gaps.

      1. I woke up very early. It was still dark, but the sky (turn) pink.
      2. A thin mist (cover) the lake.
      3. Two fishermen (sit) in a small boat.
      4. One of them (drink) tea.
      5. The other one (sing) quietly.
      6. A cold wind (blow), but I wasn’t cold in my warm jacket.
