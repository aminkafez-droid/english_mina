Five meanings of get

6 min · Read the examples, then do Exercise A

a
receive
I got a message from Lena.
b
buy
Mum got some bread.
c
catch (transport)
I got the bus to school.
d
arrive
We got home at ten.
e
become
I'm getting tired.
CCQs к примерам:
a — Did Lena send it to me? (Yes) Did I pay for it? (No)
b — Did Mum go to a shop? (Yes) Did she pay? (Yes)
c — Did I travel on the bus? (Yes) Did I buy the bus? (No!)
d — Were we at home at ten? (Yes)
e — Was I tired an hour ago? (No / a little) Is it changing now? (Yes)
Ловушка: got home без to, но got to school / to the hall.

    Exercise B. Which form of get?

    5 min · Look at the time words. Write got, gets, is getting, did you get, didn't get…

    5 мин, controlled practice. Соня печатает сама. Перед ответом пусть назовёт слово-маркер времени (yesterday, now, every day…). После каждого ответа спрашивайте: Which meaning, a–e?
    Ответы: 1 got (a) · 2 is getting (e) · 3 do you get (d) · 4 got (d) · 5 gets (e) · 6 Did you get (c) · 7 didn't get (a).
    CCQ к 2: Is it dark now? Was it dark at this time in summer? (процесс изменения → is getting)

      Read the story

      9 min · Read, then do the tasks

      9 мин. Сначала вопрос 1 (общий смысл), затем остальные. Подсветка — проверка после того, как Соня найдёт слова сама. Можно начать с фото: Where are they? How do they feel?
      CCQs: Is the story about the past or the future? Which tense do we use? How many paragraphs? Where does she say how she felt: at the start or at the end?
      Ответы: 1 — No, but they got a prize; proud. 2 — went, woke, got, was, were, started, forgot, slipped, fell, didn't win, felt (неправильные: всё, кроме started, slipped). 3 — got to (d), got cold (e), got a prize (a). 4 — nervous, amazing, embarrassing, proud, tiring, exciting.

      My first dance competition

      Paragraph 1 is about…

      Last May I went to a folk dance competition with my dance group. We woke up at six o'clock and got to the concert hall at nine. I was really nervous because there were a lot of people in the audience.

      Paragraph 2 is about…

      Before our dance I got very cold and my hands were shaking. Then the music started and I forgot about everything. It was amazing! But in the middle of the dance I slipped and nearly fell over. How embarrassing!

      Paragraph 3 is about…

      In the end we didn't win, but we got a prize for the best costumes. I felt really proud of my group. It was a tiring but exciting day.

      Tasks

      1. Did the group win? How did the writer feel at the end?
      2. Find 8 verbs in the past simple. Which are irregular?
      3. Find 3 sentences with get. What does get mean in each one (a–e)?
      4. Find the adjectives about feelings. Which end in -ing?
      5. Choose what each paragraph is about.
