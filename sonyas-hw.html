(−)I / she / they + didn't + verbI didn't go out last night.
(?)Did + you / he + verb …?Did Harry text you? Yes, he did. / No, he didn't.
bewasn't / weren't · Was…? / Were…?Was Joe late? Yes, he was.
couldcouldn't · Could…?I couldn't see the screen.

После did и didn't глагол всегда в начальной форме: прошлое уже показывает «did».

I didn't went. → I didn't go.    Did you saw? → Did you see?    Did she tired? → Was she tired?

5

-ed / -ing adjectives · How + adjective

7 min

Remember

-ed = how a person feels (что я чувствую): I'm bored. I'm disappointed.

-ing = what makes us feel this (что вызывает чувство): This film is boring. My result is disappointing.
